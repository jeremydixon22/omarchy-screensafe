# Seasons

One original paper-cut landscape: a tree beside a lake, a winding path, a small
cottage, evergreen woods and distant mountains. Those landmarks stay in the same
places in every season.

**Season** chooses Spring blossom, Summer green, Fall gold or Winter snow.
**Breeze** changes foliage and reed movement; **Seasonal detail** enables falling
petals, butterflies, leaves or snow. **Tone** chooses the natural seasonal colors
or the current theme. Theme also exposes ScreenSafe's shared Color picker.

Water, clouds and birds move gently throughout a 32-second cycle at 24 FPS. The
landscape covers the display, with cropping on other aspect ratios. It is eligible
for the existing opt-in prepared-loop system.

All artwork is original ScreenSafe vector data under the repository's MIT
license. Reproduce it with `python3 tools/seasons/build.py packs/seasons` from the
repository root. No photographs, third-party images, extra fonts, renderer
changes, new dependencies or additional sandbox permissions are needed.
