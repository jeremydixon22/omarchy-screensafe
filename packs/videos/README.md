# Videos (local prototype)

Your local clips in Full screen, Whole, Film, CRT Fit, CRT Box or Flatscreen styles, with Original, Greyscale, Sepia and Theme tones. Choose shuffle or in-order playback and Full length or a fixed interval.

**Loop** is on by default: the playlist repeats. Turn it off to play the playlist once and hold the final video's last frame. Film transports four neighboring video frames downward, with later frames at the top, slow narrow side-to-side drift and a warm projector beam and visible lamp glow with gentle flicker. CRT Fit fills the curved tube, cropping the sides of widescreen video; CRT Box keeps the whole picture with black bars as needed. Both use a walnut-and-metal cabinet, tuned dials, an Omarchy lightning badge, faint scanlines and an aperture mask. Flatscreen adds a brushed-metal bezel and stand. With Loop off, the film transport, weave and projector flicker stop at the end; the independent OLED drift continues.

Video surrounds decode and resize asynchronously before playback begins. The device is shown with black glass for a quarter second, then the media clock starts; loading never consumes the opening shot. Surround artwork appears at full opacity. CRT powers on from a restrained center line into the picture, then briefly shows horizontal tuning interference when the selected video changes. Repeating the same video does not retune. These effects use existing declarative drawing primitives and remain subject to the unchanged flash guard. Flatscreen has a slower, uneven startup: the power indicator is dark for 0.8 s, lights over 0.25 s, then an original Omarchy wordmark and opaque three-face maze cube animate in and hold. The logo fades completely by 6.05 s; a short black pause precedes the input label at 6.15 s. Playback begins at 6.8 s, and the label fades away by 8.15 s. The opening frame is held under the black boot screen, so this wait does not skip the start of the video. Startup happens once; looping does not reboot the display. **HDMI1** means the prepared video has at least 720 vertical pixels; **COMP1** means fewer. The importer caps playback at 720 lines, so higher-resolution originals use their prepared playback resolution.

Import an MP4 using `tools/videos/import.py` and place the prepared `.ssv` file in `~/Videos/Screensaver`. Ordinary MP4 files are not imported automatically in this prototype. Playback is silent, at up to 720p and 24 FPS. No example video is bundled.

See [the prototype notes](../../docs/VIDEO-PROTOTYPE.md) for import commands, limits and safety details. Full length currently uses a common interval equal to the longest clip; shorter clips hold their final frame until the next selection.

Film, CRT and Flatscreen also shift together with their video by up to 10 output pixels on each axis, with slow 17-minute and 23-minute cycles. This adds to the film's existing weave and drift. It uses output geometry rather than a fixed monitor size and keeps the curvature and image-size caches intact. It may reduce static-image exposure; it is not a guarantee against OLED burn-in.

Theme tone also offers the current theme’s swatches and a custom color. The tint keeps highlights and shadows legible by preserving the palette inks’ relative luminance. Original, Greyscale and Sepia keep their existing appearance.
